## 1. What Baylee Central is

Baylee is a free, non-commercial fan project: a card-game client, game
servers ("gateways") and Baylee Central. Baylee Central gives you:

- an account to sign in to the Baylee client and the website, and to play
  on gateways that accept Baylee accounts;
- encrypted storage for your settings, your list of gateways, deck backups
  and your chat history;
- a public directory of gateways, where you can list and manage your own.

The service is free. There are no paid features, no ads and no sale of data.
Baylee is in a test phase; features may change, fail or be removed. There is
no entitlement to any particular availability.

## 2. Your account

1. **A pseudonym is enough.** An account needs only a username of your

   computer needs no account at all.
2. **Age.** You must be at least 16. If you are not yet of age, you need the
   consent of your parents or other guardians for an account.
3. **Username and display name** must not infringe anyone's rights,
   impersonate anyone, or be insulting, discriminatory or offensive. We may
   have names that break this rule changed, or block them.
4. **Credentials.** Keep your password, your recovery code and your second
   sign-in factor (if set up) secret. We do not know your password: sign-in
   uses a method (OPAQUE) in which your password never reaches our server.

## 3. Encrypted data and recovery

Your settings, deck backups and chat history are encrypted on your device
before they reach us. We cannot read them. As a result:

- If you forget your password, you can regain access through your e-mail
  address or with our help. **Your encrypted data survives only if you have
  your recovery code or are still signed in on a device.** Otherwise it is
  lost, and nobody, including us, can bring it back.
- Keep your recovery code safe.
